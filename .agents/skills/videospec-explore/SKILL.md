# Explore a video direction

Keep this read-only. Establish audience, platform, desired viewer change, duration, success signal, and constraints; research reliable evidence, counterevidence, visual leads, and risks. For open-ended exploration, generate an angle slate of 8–12 materially different directions. For each, state title/promise, one-sentence judgment, audience tension, evidence feasibility, and why the viewer may click. When the user has already chosen a direction or supplied a protected script, assess that direction instead of generating competing topics. Select or confirm one focused direction with title, hook, selling points, a retention hypothesis, source locators, and a proposal-ready handoff that `videospec-propose` can reuse without repeating the angle search. Do not create a production, alter history, or automatically bring in another episode's working context.
