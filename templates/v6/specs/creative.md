# Creative Standards

## Purpose

The current source of truth for tone, narrative, and brand voice.

## Standards

### Standard: Intent remains legible

The production SHALL communicate one primary idea to the stated audience.

#### Check: Acceptance

- **WHEN** the production reaches final review
- **THEN** a human reviewer SHALL confirm this standard

### Standard: Chinese narration sounds like a creator speaking to one viewer

For newly authored Chinese narration, the production SHOULD open with a concrete action or consequence, state a useful judgment within 15 seconds, and carry the explanation through natural questions, tension, examples, and payoffs. It SHOULD avoid news copy, official-neutral phrasing, and repeated numbered lecture openings. Delivery and pauses SHOULD serve the viewer's comprehension rather than a fixed energy level.

#### Check: Script review

- **WHEN** the narration reaches content review
- **THEN** the reviewer SHALL assess these qualities against the stated audience and duration

### Standard: Complex information has processing time

When a new concept, consequential number, comparison, chart, or multi-step process appears, the production SHALL give viewers a clear opportunity to read or hear it before introducing the next information-bearing change. The storyboard and edit SHOULD use purposeful holds or pauses and avoid stacking several new visual, spoken, and motion cues at the same instant unless that simultaneity serves a clear narrative purpose. This standard has no fixed cut interval or universal seconds-per-screen quota. See [W3C's guidance to pause between topics](https://www.w3.org/WAI/media/av/av-content/).

#### Check: Comprehension pacing

- **WHEN** the script and storyboard are planned and the playable preview and final render are reviewed
- **THEN** the agent SHALL inspect information-dense beats in continuous playback at the target size, record any point where the next claim arrives before the current one can be understood, and revise the hold, pause, or sequence
